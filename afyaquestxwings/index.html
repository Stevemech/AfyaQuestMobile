Demo cheat sheet

A 90-second story for Tuesday.

Open with the Welcome screen to lock in the co-brand and tone — calm, but unmistakably urgent. Then walk the room through one full save-the-life loop: triage → action plan → live case.

  • Two emergencies, one platform. Pregnancy and contraception sit side by side on the hub — same triage rigor, different protocols.
  • Severity is computed live. Tap symptoms in the wizard and the meter at the bottom updates — that becomes the badge on the next screen.
  • The right facility, not the nearest. Map filters by capability — "MgSO₄" badge means the clinic can actually treat what we found.
  • The CHV is never alone. Live case keeps the receiving doctor in the loop — chat, vitals, ETA — until handoff.
